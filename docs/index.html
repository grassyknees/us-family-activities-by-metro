Family activities by U.S. metro area

Weekly counts of kids' and family events, classes and places across 42 U.S. metro areas, with the share of upcoming events that are free and the median price of paid ones. Every listing behind these numbers is researched from the organizer's own page and re-checked daily by Grassy Knees. The table holds totals only, never the listings themselves.

Findings

    By metro area

    Counts describe what Grassy Knees lists, not every event in a metro, and coverage depth differs by metro: compare shares and medians rather than raw counts. "Next 30 days" is relative to the snapshot date. Prices are the organizer's published price, usually one child's ticket. Click a column heading to sort; click a metro to see its current listings on grassyknees.com.
